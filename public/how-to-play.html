How to Play Token Quest

Your complete guide to the dungeon

🚀 Getting Started

Token Quest is a free browser idle RPG — no download, no install. Just open the game and start fighting.

  1. Open tokenquest in your browser
  2. Create your hero — choose a username, password, and class
  3. Your account is saved to the cloud automatically
  4. Start attacking bosses and collecting gear!
💡 Your account is cloud-saved. You can log in from any device using your username and password.

⚡ The Energy System

Energy is your attack fuel. Every attack costs energy. Manage it wisely!

  • Base energy cap: 180
  • Boots gear can increase your cap up to +50
  • Energy regenerates 1 point every 6 minutes
  • Legendary Boots give +5% regen speed bonus
  • Watch a short ad to earn +5 energy instantly
💡 Boss kills also drop energy (80% chance) — between 4 and 15 points per kill!

⚔️ Combat System

Bosses get progressively harder — Boss #1 has 1 HP, Boss #2 has 2 HP, and so on. Your damage depends on your gear.

Attack Modes

  • x1 Attack — costs 1 energy, deals base damage
  • x5 Attack — costs 5 energy, deals 5x damage in one strike

Critical Hits

Critical hits deal double damage. Your crit chance comes from gear — Legendary Sword gives 5% crit, Legendary Leggings give an additional 5%. If you crit on an x5 attack, ALL 5 hits are critical!

Damage Formula

Total damage = (1 base STR + weapon dmg + glove dmg) × attack mode × crit multiplier × leggings bonus

💡 Example: With a Legendary Sword (dmg 5) + Legendary Gloves (dmg 5): base damage = 1+5+5 = 11. x5 attack = 55 damage. Critical x5 = 110 damage!

🏆 Social Features

Leaderboard

Global rankings updated in real time. Players are ranked by total XP. Click any player to view their profile, gear, and inventory.

Friends

Add friends by their exact username. View their profile, gear loadout, and latest items. Friends show up in your friends list for quick access.

Gift System

Spend 100 tokens to send a random item from your inventory to a friend. They receive it in their inbox and can claim it instantly. The sender doesn't know which item gets sent — it's a surprise!

💡 Earn 5 tokens per boss kill. Save up 100 tokens to send your first gift!

📈 Progression Tips

  • Focus on getting a Legendary Sword first — it's the biggest damage increase
  • Use x5 attacks when you have full energy for maximum efficiency
  • Watch ads when energy is low — free +5 energy every 30 seconds
  • Check back every hour — energy regens even when you're offline
  • Equip Boots as soon as you get them — more energy cap = more attacks per session
  • Log in daily to maintain your streak
